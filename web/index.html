How the expected payout is worked out

expected payout = gross value
                - restaurant-funded discount
                - commission  (agreed % x commissionable base)
                - payment-gateway fee
                - packaging   (only if the restaurant bears it)
                - tax withheld (GST TCS + s.194-O TDS)

The commissionable base is gross value - restaurant-funded discount, defined in one place in the code and used by every rule. The platform-funded discount is deliberately not deducted: when the platform buys a promotion for the customer, you sold at full price, and commission belongs on full price. A statement that charges commission on the pre-discount value while paying out on the post-discount value is the commonest way a payout goes wrong.

A commission overcharge is reported separately, but it is not added to the money owed back: because the expected payout already uses your agreed rate, every rupee of excess commission is inside the short payment. Claiming it twice would overstate the claim.

Rates, constants and what to confirm yourself
